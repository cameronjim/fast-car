# Parallel parking and three-point turn, 2026-10-07

Roadmap 2.9, owner-requested. A new node, `racer_control/park_node`, finds a parking slot with
the LiDAR while driving slowly along a row of obstacles, plans the classic two-arc parallel
park, and drives it from the VESC's wheel odometry (`/odom/wheel`). The same node does a
three-point turn in the lane it stands in. It is an interim, odometry-only version: the
roadmap's 2.9 line asks for a Reeds-Shepp planner on top of localization (2.4 / 2.5), which
does not exist yet, so this does NOT tick 2.9.

**THE NODE DRIVES THE CAR, INCLUDING IN REVERSE, ON ITS OWN** once it is started. Nothing has
been run on the car. Sim and unit tests only (see "Tests").

## Pieces

| File | What |

| `include/racer_control/park_slot.hpp` | slot detection and lane measurement from one scan |
| `include/racer_control/park_planner.hpp` | the two-arc parallel park and the three-point turn |
| `include/racer_control/park_controller.hpp` | the state machine, dead reckoning, stop lead, blocked abort, odometry fallback |

| `launch/park.launch.py` | `profile` argument, `floor-2026-10-07` profile |

All of the logic is ROS-free and gtest-covered; the node is plumbing. Every physical constant
comes from the generated vehicle_params binding (CLAUDE.md invariant 2). The node publishes
`/drive_raw` only and reads the gated `/drive`; it never publishes `/drive` (invariant 1).

## Geometry

Frames are REP-103 and the pose is the rear axle centre, like safety_node's arc corridor. With
the committed vehicle_params (schema 0.12.0):

- steering on the arcs: delta = `park_steering_fraction` x `steering.max_angle_rad` =
  0.95 x 0.4189 = 0.397955 rad, leaving the servo some travel short of its stop
- turning radius of the rear axle: R = L / tan(delta) = 0.3302 / 0.420385 = **0.7855 m**
- the slot centreline sits at the row face + `chassis.width_m` / 2 + margin, so the parked car's
  near side is `margin_m` (0.1 m) inside the row line
- lateral offset d = row lateral distance + width / 2 + margin, measured from the car's
  lateral position while it drives parallel to the row
- arc angle: cos(theta) = 1 - d / (2 R)
- the two arcs together move the rear axle 2 R sin(theta) back along the row and d sideways

For a **0.5 m lateral offset**: cos(theta) = 0.681724, **theta = 0.8207 rad (47.02 deg)**, each
arc R theta = 0.6446 m, **2 R sin(theta) = 1.1493 m** along the row. (0.5 m is less than the
0.51 m the margins need, width + 2 x margin, so the planner itself would refuse it as "too close
to the row"; the numbers are the geometry.) One pass reaches at most d = 2 R = 1.571 m.

The manoeuvre, for a slot on the right (s = -1; the left is the mirror image):

1. DRIVE_TO_START: straight, parallel to the row, to the start x.
2. ARC_1: reverse at `park_speed_mps` with the steering toward the slot (s x delta) through
   theta: the rear swings into the pocket, the nose out, heading -s x theta.
3. ARC_2: reverse with the opposite lock through theta: back to parallel.
4. STRAIGHTEN: steering to 0, then forward by up to `final_forward_m` to centre the car.

Placement: the planner tries final rear-axle positions from the rearmost (rear bumper `margin`
ahead of the slot's near edge) forward in 1 cm steps and takes the first that passes every
check. The start is then the final x + 2 R sin(theta): the rear axle is ahead of the slot's far
edge by 2 R sin(theta) minus the share of the slot ahead of the final rear axle.

Checks, for every motion including DRIVE_TO_START and the forward move:

- SWEPT BODY: the bounding box about the rear axle, x in [-rear_overhang, wheelbase +
  front_overhang], |y| <= width / 2 (so both front corners and the inside rear corner, and the
  edges between them), sampled every 1 cm of rear-axle travel, must keep `margin_m` from every
  obstacle segment: the row face either side of the pocket, the pocket's two end faces and its
  back, and the opposite wall if the scan saw one.
- GATE: safety_node's obstacle gate is re-stated in `park_geometry.hpp` for planning (not shared
  code: layer 3 must not share a code path with the controllers it gates). Going forward it is
  the corridor from the LiDAR head, reversing the rear corridor from the rear bumper line, along
  the steering arc, with the body band, the outer-corner band and its horizon and the outer
  sector; the distance must stay above max(`limits.min_forward_clearance_m`, |speed| x
  `limits.ttc_brake_s`) + `gate_margin_m` = max(0.25, 0.15) + 0.03 = 0.28 m. This is what
  sets the rear gap: at the end of ARC_2 the request still has a lock on, so the rear corridor
  measures the rear obstacle's face along the ARC and reads it nearer than the straight gap.
  Without this check the plan would put the rear bumper `margin` (0.1 m) from the rear
  obstacle, well inside that 0.28 m, and the rear corridor would brake ARC_2 before it ends
  (`test_park_planner.cpp` GatePredictionRejectsASlotTheBodyFits has a slot the body fits that
  the prediction rejects); with it the rear gap after ARC_2 is about 0.37 m, and STRAIGHTEN then
  moves the car forward (forward requests are judged on the front corridor).

Rejections, each logged with its numbers: car not parallel to the row (the detector's row
angle, or the dead-reckoned heading, beyond 0.087 rad), car's side nearer the row than the
margin, pocket shallower than width + 2 x margin (0.51 m), d > 2 R, slot shorter than the car
plus two margins, every placement touching the swept-body check (the log names the motion and
the face), or, with the body clear, the gate check. A rejected slot is skipped and SEARCH carries
on.

**Required slot length** (the shortest slot the planner accepts, `minimum_slot_length_m`, row
face 0.3 to 0.6 m out, pocket 0.6 to 0.8 m deep): **about 1.51 m**, 2.6 car lengths, almost
independent of the row distance. Without the gate check it would be about 1.24 m; the 0.27 m
difference is safety_node's 0.25 m floor on the rear arc. The detector's own floor,
`slot_length_min_m` = `chassis.length_m` + 0.35 = 0.93 m, only decides what counts as a pocket;
anything between that and 1.51 m is found, planned, and rejected as too short.

### Three-point turn

The lane is the nearest return left and right over the body length (plus 0.3 m each end), the
walls modelled as straight lines parallel to the car. Turning left: TURN_1 forward at s x delta
through the largest angle (up to pi) that keeps the margin and the forward gate clear; TURN_2
reverse at the opposite lock through the largest angle (up to pi - theta_1) likewise; TURN_3
forward through the rest, which must be clear for its whole angle (TURN_2 is shortened step by
step until it is). A lane wide enough for theta_1 = pi is a U-turn, one arc. The narrowest lane,
car centred, is **1.60 m** (1.36 m without the gate check). In a 1.8 m lane: 54.7 + 56.8 + 68.5
deg. Narrower lanes are refused before anything moves.

## Slot detection

`park_slot.hpp`, every scan during SEARCH. The returns on the parking side between
`chassis.width_m` / 2 and `lateral_max_m` (2 m) to the side, from 1.5 m behind to 2.5 m ahead of
the rear axle, go into 5 cm bins along the row, each keeping its nearest lateral distance; the
mount yaw (pi on this car) and the mount position are applied first. A bin with no return is
open (the pocket's interior seen past an obstacle edge reads like that), short holes between
returns are filled, the row face is the 25th percentile of the bins, and a pocket is a run of
bins at least 0.1 m beyond it, bounded by obstacle bins on both sides inside the window (an
unbounded run is not a slot yet). It must be `slot_depth_min_m` deep (default width + 2 x margin)
and `slot_length_min_m` long. The row's angle to the car is fitted from the face bins; more than
0.087 rad refuses the scan. Three consecutive scans must agree on both edges within 0.1 m in the
odometry frame; the confirmed slot is their average, and its edges and depth are logged in the
vehicle frame at detection.

## State machine

```
IDLE --~/start--> SEARCH --slot confirmed--> SLOT_FOUND --plan feasible--> DRIVE_TO_START
  --> ARC_1 --> ARC_2 --> STRAIGHTEN --> DONE
SLOT_FOUND --plan rejected--> SEARCH (slot ignored from then on)
IDLE --~/three_point_turn--> TURN_1 --> TURN_2 --> TURN_3 --> DONE
any active phase --~/abort, held by safety_node, odometry lost, re-check failed--> ABORT
```

Every transition is logged at INFO (ABORT at WARN, with the phase and the reason). Each step
holds one steering and runs STOPPING (zero speed until the wheel speed is below
`stopped_speed_mps` twice), STEERING (the request ramps to the new steering while the car
stands), SETTLING (`settle_s` with zero speed so the servo finishes first), MOVING. The steering
only ever changes while the car stands. When MOVING starts, the step's target is resolved
against the dead-reckoned pose: ARC_1 and ARC_2 end on headings (so ARC_2 ends parallel even if
ARC_1 overshot), STRAIGHTEN on the planned final x, and the swept body of the resolved motion is
checked again (half the margin) or the run aborts. The distance the car rolls after each stop
command is measured and sent early on the next step in the same direction (the stop lead, at
most 0.15 m; DRIVE_TO_START's forward stop seeds ARC_1's).

DONE and ABORT publish zero for `final_hold_s` (1 s), then nothing: safety_node brakes on the
silence. While a nonzero speed is requested, a gated `/drive` of zero (safety_node braking) or
no fresh gated `/drive` for `blocked_abort_s` (2 s) aborts. SEARCH aborts after
`search_max_distance_m` (6 m) without a feasible slot.

Dead reckoning: the pose advances by the signed `/odom/wheel` distance on the arc of the current
steering request. ASSUMED: that distance is the rear axle's arc length, and the turning radius
is L / tan(delta) with the steering map linear (`steering.pwm_to_angle_table` is null). With
`require_odometry` true (the car's profile) a run refuses to start without fresh odometry and
aborts if it goes stale; with false (sim fixtures) it integrates the gated `/drive` speed, WARNs
once per second and sets `odom_fallback=true` in `~/status`.

## Interface

- Services (`std_srvs/srv/Trigger`): `/park_node/start`, `/park_node/three_point_turn`,
  `/park_node/abort`. The response carries the reason when refused.
- Subscribes `/scan` (best_effort), `/odom/wheel` (reliable), the gated `/drive` (reliable, read
  only). Publishes `/drive_raw` (reliable, 50 Hz while running) and `~/status` (`std_msgs/String`,
  `key=value` pairs, 2 Hz and on every transition; recorded by `car_teleop.launch.py`'s bag).
- Planning runs off the control thread (`std::async`), so the 50 Hz `/drive_raw` never pauses
  for it (safety_node's watchdog brakes after 3 missed cycles).

Parameters (node default; the `floor-2026-10-07` profile in bold where it sets one):

| Parameter | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `park_side` | right (**right**) | side of the row to park on |
| `search_speed_mps` | 0.5 (**0.5**) | SEARCH and every forward move |
| `park_speed_mps` | -0.5 (**-0.5**) | every reverse move |
| `park_steering_fraction` | 0.95 (**0.95**) | arc steering as a fraction of `steering.max_angle_rad` |

## Running it on the car

Prerequisites, all open:

1. **VESC UART wired and `/odom/wheel` checked** (runbook "VESC telemetry", V1 to V5). Without
   it the floor profile refuses to start, by design. Do the 10-turn distance check: the arcs are
   measured in that distance.
2. **The two overhangs measured**: `chassis.front_overhang_m` and `chassis.rear_overhang_m` are
   PROVISIONAL placeholders (0.13 / 0.12 m). The swept-body check and the rear corridor use them.
3. Measure the real turning radius at `park_steering_fraction` 0.95 (drive a full circle at
   0.5 m/s, both locks, and compare with 0.786 m): the steering map is assumed linear.
4. The L6 wheels-off-ground sweep for the new node (wheels turn the right way in each phase,
   steering changes only with the wheels stopped), two-person rule, kill switch in the second
   person's hand.

Then, wheels on the floor, a row of boxes on the right with a gap of at least 1.6 m and 0.6 m
deep, the car parallel to the row about 0.3 to 0.5 m from it, the space around and behind it
clear:

```sh
# 1. The stack, with the LiDAR and the VESC telemetry, no teleop source:
exec ros2 launch racer_bringup car_teleop.launch.py lidar:=true vesc:=true
# 2. Second terminal, into the same container:
ros2 launch racer_control park.launch.py profile:=floor-2026-10-07
# 3. Third terminal:
ros2 service call /park_node/start std_srvs/srv/Trigger
#    or, in a lane at least 1.6 m wide:
ros2 service call /park_node/three_point_turn std_srvs/srv/Trigger
#    to stop it:
ros2 service call /park_node/abort std_srvs/srv/Trigger
```

The startup line `park_node up: ...` must show `require_odometry true`, `R 0.7855 m` and
`laser yaw 3.1416 rad`. **The car reverses on its own** after `SLOT_FOUND`; the kill switch is
the first thing to reach for. Every transition is in the log and `/park_node/status`, both in the
run's bag. Ctrl-C on park.launch.py stops it too (safety_node brakes on the silence).

### L5 sim-in-loop

`racer_gym_bridge` gained four opt-in parameters (all default off, every existing caller
unchanged):

- `pocket_start_m`, `pocket_length_m`, `pocket_depth_m`, `pocket_side`: a recess cut into one
  wall of the walled corridor, measured along the raceline as driven
  (`track_loader.carve_pocket`).
- `publish_wheel_odom`: `/odom/wheel` in vesc_odometry_node's shape from the gym's longitudinal
  speed (in the gym's kinematic regime exactly the rear axle's speed), integrated per step.
- `lidar_offset_x_m`, `lidar_yaw_rad`, `lidar_fov_rad`, `lidar_num_beams`: `/scan` cast by a
  second `ScanSimulator2D` from a mounted LiDAR instead of the gym pose. The canary passes the
  car's mount (0.285 m ahead of the rear axle, the gym pose being the CG 0.171 m ahead of it),
  yaw pi, 360 degrees at 0.72 degrees, all read from vehicle_params. So park_node and safety_node
  run with the car's own LiDAR geometry from the binding, and the rear corridor sees straight
  behind the car, which the gym's 270 degree scan from the CG does not.
- `mirror_reverse_speed_control`: f1tenth_gym's speed controller brakes a reversing car with a
  gain of 2 a_max / v_max where a forward one gets 10 a_max / -v_min, 20 times weaker: at
  -0.5 m/s it rolls on about 0.53 m after a zero command. That is a model artefact no
  manoeuvre can absorb (ARC_1 would turn 39 degrees too far). The bridge drives the gym in
  acceleration mode with the forward gains applied to the speed's magnitude.

The scene: a 14 x 4.5 m loop with a 1.2 m lane; on the bottom straight a 1.8 m x 0.7 m pocket in
the right wall 1.6 m ahead of the start. bridge_node, safety_node (car defaults) and park_node
(the floor profile, `require_odometry` true) on the real command path. PASS: DONE, final pose
parallel within 3 degrees and the whole body inside the pocket, the body's clearance from the
nominal walls above the map's 0.025 m half-cell at every ground-truth sample, no gym collision,
a reverse command through the gated `/drive`, and no ttc / ttc_reverse brake from safety_node.

Measured locally (ros-dev, Apple silicon, Docker), three runs: slot detected at 0.70 to 2.45 m
ahead of the rear axle, plan d 0.911 to 0.918 m, theta 65.2 to 65.4 deg, final rear axle
(9.241, -0.928, +0.75 deg), (9.252, -0.935, -0.62 deg) and (9.244, -0.924, -0.58 deg) in a
pocket spanning x 8.60 to 10.40 and y -1.30 to -0.60, minimum body clearance 0.204 to 0.215 m,
no obstacle brake; the only
safety events were the watchdog on park_node's idle silence before the start and after DONE.
The first run also had seven `rate_limit` warnings (two park_node steps landing in one
safety_node cycle); the ramp fractions were lowered to 0.25 / 0.35 and the later runs had none.

Sim artefacts, stated: the gym's ray marcher reads a wall seen square-on about 0.04 m too far
(pixel-quantised distance transform), so the measured row face was 0.67 m against 0.6 m and the
car parked about 7 cm deeper than planned; the corridor walls are quantised to 0.05 m cells.
The gym's body for its own collision check is wheelbase-long and blind behind, which is why the
canary checks contact itself with the vehicle_params box.
